⬡ Hexphys Frontier — guide

An open continent of ~600,000 hexes. Rain falls, rivers run, lakes fill and drain with the seasons, forests spread where the soil is wet and burn when it is dry. Nothing is scripted. You found a settlement in the fog and grow it.
How a day works (1 day ≈ 1 s at 1×; a year is 300 days)
  • Each settler eats 0.06 food/day. While food > 15 and there is free housing, the population grows about 1% per day (plus a trickle of newcomers) — it doubles roughly every 70 days, so housing and food run out fast. With no food, settlers starve at the same rate.
  • Workers: farms, lumber camps and quarries need 2 settlers, mines 3. Settlers are assigned by the Workforce priority list (top first); buildings that miss out sit idle with a grey ring. Reorder or pause types there, or pause single buildings in the inspector.
  • Housing: camp 12, outpost 4, house 6. The top bar shows each resource's net income per day; hover it for the breakdown.
  • The advisor line under the top bar names your current bottleneck.
What each building needs
  • Farm: food = 1.6 × (average soil moisture within 2 hexes − 15%) / 85% per day. Check the moisture lens. Standing water >0.15 m floods it: no output, damage.
  • Lumber camp: cuts forest within 6 hexes (>25% cover) → up to ~1 wood/day + a little resin. Forest regrows. See the forest lens.
  • Quarry: 0.7 stone/day, only on ground ≥ 30 m (the height lens).
  • Mine: 0.6 ore/day from known deposits within 2 hexes; deposits run out. Find them by prospecting (the ore lens shows where you have looked).
  • Outpost: claims land within 16 hexes and reveals 30. You can only build inside your territory (the bright border).
Water is terrain
  • No pipes or dams as objects. Dig lowers land 0.6 m per stroke, Raise builds it up. A raised bank holds water up to its material's strength (stone 2.5 m) and erodes if the head across it is larger. The Forge turns ferrite, silica and resin into stronger bank materials.
Pan: drag (Inspect tool), right-drag, or WASD/arrows · Zoom: wheel or +/- · Click a building to inspect it · Space pause · Esc back to Inspect
Forge — lattice chemistry
Atoms swap places at temperature T; bonds lower energy. Anneal (cool slowly) for strong, ordered material; quench for fast, porous, weak. Silica bonds hardest to ferrite; resin is soft and tough.
