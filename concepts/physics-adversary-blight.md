# physics-adversary-blight

> **Parked idea.** User note from 2026-11-20, logged before playtesting
> Frontier (docs/frontier.html). Revisit after the playtest, and expand it
> only with the user.

## Definition

The open world's adversary is not an AI faction but something that grows
in the environment: a spreading physical or chemical process such as a
mold, blight or runaway reaction. The player races it against time.

## The idea as stated

- It is just physics or a chemical reaction, with no scripted antagonist.
- It can overwhelm you.
- It destroys resources and damages the landscape.
- If you beat it back, you can reshape the land to produce the resources
  you need.
- Over time you can cultivate the landscape to reverse the damage, like
  tending a garden while fighting back a mold.
- Open questions from the user: it might not work out, and it might be
  relevant to only one level of the ladder.
