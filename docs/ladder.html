Rung 2 · Device — top-down continuum (MPM)

Each card is now a continuum material: stiffness, toughness and density come from the board you sealed. Paint defences, then release the flood. Water that reaches the village floods it; water that reaches the sea drains. Nothing holds things down but ground friction, so dense material grips harder and costs more.
