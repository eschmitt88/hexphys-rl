⬡ Hexphys Watershed

One island, simulated whole. Storms drift across it on the wind and rain where they pass; the land decides where that water runs. A volcano leaks lava, and now and then it erupts. You shape the land so water and lava end up where you want them — and export both. Use the flow lens to see where rivers really run: they carry their whole catchment's rain.
The goal
  • Export. Water counts when it reaches an Export gate (⇲ on the coast; up to 20/day each). It is worth 1 in the wet season, 3 in the dry — so storing water behind dams and releasing it in the dry season pays. No river goes there by itself: you dig channels to bring it, and dams to store it for the dry season. Lava (worth 3) is caught by lava traps — pits that swallow an eruption's flow, ladled out by workers before it freezes; obsidian (worth 10) forms where lava is quenched by water — so bring water to the lava, or lava to the water. Each year has an export charter (top right).
Earthworks — say what you want, settlers dig it
  • Channel: click start, then end. It always runs downhill from the higher end, cutting through rises.
  • Dam: click one valley side, then the other. Flat crest at the lower side's height; water pools behind. Give it a spillway, or storms overtop it and wear an earth dam down. Obsidian dams resist 9× better.
  • Levee: a 1.5 m bank along a line, to keep water — or lava — out. Basin: a pond to catch water.
  • A yellow outline previews the plan with its cost in earth and days. Cuts give earth; fills use it.
  • Dams hold a limited head: earth holds ~2.5 m (more if wider), obsidian ~22 m. Click a dam to see the water against it. Beyond its limit it sags and fails.
  • Hired crews (Workforce) turn surplus food into extra diggers for big projects.
Settlers
  • Each eats 0.06 food/day; the town grows ~1%/day while food > 15 and housing is free. Farms yield by soil moisture.
  • Workforce: rows fill top-down; Earthworks takes everyone left at its row. Each digger moves 1.5 earth/day.
The volcano
  • Vents leak a little lava always. Before an eruption a vent rumbles for days — check the heat lens and move what's in the way. Lava buries buildings (the camp just stops working; move it). Lava cooled in air becomes basalt; quenched in water, obsidian. A Quench works fed by both a lava path and a water channel makes obsidian steadily — design its insides in the Works (the level below). Digging deep into hot ground can open a new vent.
